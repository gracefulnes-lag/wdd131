Congo Cultural Trails

A local travel guide celebrating culture, routes, and stories from the Republic of the Congo.

Website Planning Document

This WDD 131 project plan defines the scope, audience, and visual direction for Congo Cultural Trails. The plan includes the site name, purpose, user scenarios, color schema, typography, and wireframe layouts for mobile and desktop views.

1. Site Name

Congo Cultural Trails

Chosen for its focus on travel and culture in the Republic of the Congo, this name highlights authentic local experiences, cultural landmarks, and guided route planning.

Optional domain idea: congoculturaltrails.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Congo Cultural Trails is to provide visitors with clear, useful information for planning safe cultural journeys in the Congo. The website will offer destination highlights, recommended travel routes, local customs, lodging and guide contact details, and event previews to help travelers explore the region respectfully and confidently.

3. Scenarios

  • Where can I find authentic cultural sites and travel routes in the Republic of the Congo?
  • How do I connect with local guides, accommodations, and safe transportation options?
  • What customs, festivals, and food should I plan for during my visit?

4. Color Schema

The selected colors convey natural landscapes, warm hospitality, and clear navigation for the travel audience.

Forest Green

Use for primary headings, navigation accents, and call-to-action buttons.

Clay Accent

Use for highlights, links, cards, and section dividers.

Soft Linen

Use for page backgrounds and content surfaces.

5. Typography

The typography supports readability and a warm, polished travel brand identity.

  • Playfair Display — headings and section titles for an elegant, inviting style.
  • Inter — body text and paragraph copy for clean readability on all screen sizes.
  • System sans-serif — fallback font for buttons, navigation, and labels.

Heading example: Explore local trails and culture

Body example: Discover authentic routes, cultural stories, and travel tips for the Congo.
